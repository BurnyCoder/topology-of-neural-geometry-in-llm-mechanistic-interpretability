How the example results were made

These are the six clouds of the README's example figure: positive, partial and negative results. Pick one and grow the radius r. The 3D view shows the alpha complex? at that radius, and the persistence diagram? records when each loop is born and filled in. Below are the Monte Carlo null that decides whether the most persistent loop is significant, and the test of whether the concept goes around the hole. Every number is read from the repository's results files. New to the method? Open Method and terms or the worked example.

The largest persistence ratio π? in each of the null clouds built to match this cloud's design?, against this cloud's own; p is the Monte Carlo p-value?.

Does the concept go around the hole once?

Each concept sits at the circular mean? of its points' hole angle θ_B?, joined in semantic order. Going around once means alignment p < 0.05? and winding number ±1?.
