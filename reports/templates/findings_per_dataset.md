# Findings per dataset

Date: 2026-11-15. Every number is generated from `results/` (`run.py report`). Compact overview: the README's evaluation tables; methods: [docs/methodology.md](../docs/methodology.md).


Here "hole" or "loop" means a significant H1 loop under the design-matched Monte Carlo null (999 draws), and "goes around" means winding exactly once in the loop's circle-valued coordinate. The nulls are Gaussian, so a significant loop means "more persistent than a covariance-matched Gaussian". That is not proof of a hole: the same test flags loops on {{audit3.mc.fpr:2}} of hole-free synthetic clouds, and on {{audit3.mc.fam.sphere_b0:2}} of spheres and caps (third audit, [exp8_audit3.md](exp8_audit3.md)). Evidence for a hole therefore comes from the traversal (tested against shuffled labels), from where the loop lives, and from the concept centres alone. For prompt-template designs, a hole counts as a ring of concepts only if it runs through the concept clusters: a loop that dies before any two clusters touch lies inside one cluster (second audit, [exp7_audit.md](exp7_audit.md)). The alignment R and its permutation p measure *ordering*; only winding ±1 carries the cyclic claim.

This shows that the model preserves the hue circle of its stimuli, not that it constructs a hue circle of its own.
