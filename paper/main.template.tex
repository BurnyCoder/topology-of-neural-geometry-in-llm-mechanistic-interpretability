\begin{abstract}
We ask whether semantic labels wind once around a detected loop in projected activations or weights. On {{sci.n_real}} datasets from a pinned shape-fitting study, fitted geometry and persistent homology use the same observations. Neither method passes the complete registered decision rule on weekdays, months or modular residues. Residues align in a three-dimensional projection and lose the required coordinate in ten principal components. The amended primary hypothesis about model steering remains untested. The fitted and persistence-based estimates agree on components, loops and voids for {{sci.h4_full}}/{{sci.n_real}} datasets. On eligible synthetic clouds, the persistence estimator achieves {{exp1.P.acc:3}} family-macro accuracy against the generator's catalog, compared with {{exp1.Bb.acc:3}} for a learned family baseline. Arcs and spherical caps near closure receive closed catalog labels. With the same predictions and eligibility, scoring the actual angular domains reverses the relative performance in both families. The results characterize these fitted objects, projected samples and benchmark conventions. They leave continuous full-space topology and causal steering unresolved.
\end{abstract}

\section{Motivation and related work}

In a projection, a small gap in an open curve can be hard to distinguish from a closed loop. A helix can also wind around its axis while remaining contractible. We examine which of these descriptions the recorded data and decision rules support. Engels et al.\ identified calendar representations and intervened on model activations~\citep{engels2025notall}. Nanda et al.\ studied frequency-selective representations in modular addition~\citep{nanda2023progress}. Both Clock and Pizza computations can have circular input embeddings~\citep{zhong2023}. Wurgaft et al.\ tested movement along fitted manifolds with intervened model outputs~\citep{wurgaft2026manifold}. Our contribution is a paired evaluation of fitted-shape and persistence criteria on a fixed corpus, with registered negative controls, synthetic comparisons and a reconstruction of each hypothesis decision. The observational readouts here cannot answer the intervention question.

Persistent cohomology supplies circular coordinates used in experimental neuroscience~\citep{desilva2011circular,rybakken2019decoding,gardner2022toroidal}. Shapes in embeddings and topology across model layers have also been studied directly~\citep{modell2025origins,gardinazzi2025persistent,moisescu2025modular}. Smooth processes and random walks can produce apparent circular organization after PCA~\citep{antognini2018pca,shinn2023phantom}. Those observations motivate explicit projection checks and comparisons with smooth-process controls.

\section{Data and methods}

The primary clouds are Llama-3.1-8B weekday and month activations and the residue embeddings of a modular-addition transformer. Days with times are confirmatory-secondary. Hue is exploratory. The other sets include ordinal values, initialization controls, Gaussian processes, random walks and toy models of superposition. Prompt rows within a concept share templates or semantic structure. \paragraph{Fitted shapes (A).}
We centre each cloud, use its top three PC scores, orient signs as in the source and normalize RMS radius to one. The fit supplies a parametrized shape with a catalog topology. Betti numbers count components, independent loops and independent two-dimensional cycles over the chosen field~\citep{hatcher2002algebraic}. An embedded nonzero-pitch helix over a finite interval is contractible. For nonsingular closed orientable connected surfaces, genus is the number of handles. Assigning genus to an arbitrary filtered Betti vector requires additional surface assumptions. Cluster and polytope targets describe centres or vertices. For a fitted-shape feature size $m$ and empirical bidirectional noise-and-coverage scale $\nu$, $\rho_A=m/(2\nu)\geq1$ defines catalog eligibility. The selected minimum of gaps or diameters is heuristic. The margin alone cannot identify the topology of a noisy offset or establish the reach conditions of a recovery theorem~\citep{niyogi2008finding}. \evidence{docs/analytic_topology.md}{Catalog and margin definitions}.

\paragraph{Persistence (B).}
GUDHI alpha persistence uses $\mathbb F_2$ in three dimensions. We take square roots of its squared filtration radii and cross-check against the direct-radius interface~\citep{gudhi}. The alpha complex models the topology of an offset at a specified radius~\citep{edelsbrunner1995union,bauer2017morse}. For finite positive H1 and H2 bars, $\pi=d/b$ measures relative persistence. The primary real-data statistic is the largest $\pi$ in each homological degree. Each of 999 draws is projected and normalized. Prompt-template designs redraw concept centres from a fitted Gaussian and retain within-group residuals. The other designs draw from a Gaussian matched to the observed covariance spectrum. The reference tail estimate is $(1+\#\{\pi_{\rm null,max}\geq\pi_{\rm obs}\})/(M+1)$. Fitted nuisance parameters limit an exact exchangeability interpretation~\citep{dufour2006}. The per-dataset conditional binomial intervals quantify simulation error. \evidence{results/science/null_replay.csv}{Fresh-draw comparisons}.

\paragraph{Coordinates and alignment.}
DREiMac selects the longest additive-lifetime landmark Rips H$^1$ class over $\mathbb F_{47}$ using at most 200 landmarks~\citep{perea2023dreimac}. The registered standard-range construction requires sufficient lifetime. We record refusal, actual open-ball landmark coverage and overlap with the alpha interval on a common radius scale. Interval overlap does not establish a generator correspondence across complexes, fields and ranking criteria. Concept mean angles $\bar\theta_c$ are compared with semantic phases $\phi_c$ by $R=\max_{s=\pm1}|n^{-1}\sum_c\exp(i(\bar\theta_c-s\phi_c))|$. Under exchangeable concept labels, enumerate small permutations or use the plus-one Monte Carlo formula~\citep{phipson2010permutation}. Repeat the residue-frequency search in each permutation. Winding sums principal increments around the designated closed label sequence. A magnitude of one permits local reversals. The binary exact-order check also requires all increments to have the same sign. \evidence{src/neurotopo/stats/circular.py}{Definitions and implementation}.

\paragraph{Design and chronology.}
H1-A requires a fitted hole, sufficient margin and aligned unit winding. H1-B requires a significant 3D alpha loop, aligned unit winding in 3D, and aligned unit winding with strict coordinates in ten PCs. Holm adjusts the six primary 3D alignment tests. Unavailable coordinates receive one for this decision and retain a missing measurement in the audit. H2 tests the two methods on ordinal and initialization controls. H3 uses day phase for days with times. H4 compares three classifiers and their fitted/persistence vector agreement. H5 compares synthetic instruments. Amendment A2, after the fitted-shape results were known and before the recorded real Method B analysis, makes H6 steering the primary hypothesis. The angular-target sensitivity, nested null calibration and chain verification are post hoc to the study. \evidence{reports/preregistration.md}{Registered design and amendments}. \evidence{reports/reviews/2026-11-26-protocol.md}{Audit protocol}.

\subsection{Reference calibration and path verification}

The synthetic alpha estimator uses the left-Gumbel approximation proposed by Bobrowski and Skraba~\citep{bobrowski2023universal}. Weak universality fixes dimension, degree and construction. A later preprint derives distinct degree-one \v{C}ech limits in two and three dimensions and excludes a left-Gumbel form in its models~\citep{weak2024,strong2026}. We therefore interpret P as an empirical score. All blob size-by-noise cells are reported. Pooled within-cloud KS distances describe departures from uniformity and do not provide calibrated iid tests. \evidence{results/science/blobs.csv}{Calibration counts, denominators and intervals}.

For the real-data Gaussian comparisons, a nested simulation used the sample sizes of the primary sets, 128 independent outer datasets and 99 freshly fitted inner draws per dataset. The six-dimensional spectrum and the within-group residual templates are specified in the audit protocol. Every marginal 95\% binomial interval includes the exchangeable strict-rank reference of 0.04. Sampling uncertainty and the restricted simulation designs preclude a general finite-sample calibration guarantee. The fresh replay reproduced the ranks in all {{sci.null_arrays}} archived alpha-null arrays. \evidence{results/science/calibration.csv}{Rejection rates and exact intervals}. \evidence{results/science/null_replay.csv}{Seed-level replay}.

For each displayed alpha cycle we checked the boundary over $\mathbb F_2$ and membership in the boundary span immediately before and at its paired death simplex~\citep{edelsbrunner2010computational}. All {{sci.rep2.total}} shells pass. Of {{sci.rep1.total}} shortest birth paths, {{sci.rep1.failed}} carry an older class through death. The explorer flags those paths. All selected loop-gallery examples pass. A valid representative remains nonunique, and an offset cavity or projected loop alone says little about a model's use of the feature. \evidence{results/science/representatives.csv.gz}{Every chain certificate}.

\section{Discussion and limitations}

A semantic cycle, a closed fitted object, a nontrivial homology class of a finite offset, and a causal intervention answer different questions. The Gaussian comparison also flags some hole-free generative designs. The positive 3D findings for hue and residues support label organization in specific coordinates. The negative registered conjunctions leave other layers, prompts, metrics, projection dimensions and training seeds open. Observations across projections reuse the same data. The higher-dimensional checks and empirical controls describe sensitivity within this corpus. They provide no independent model replication. The calibration simulations examine one spectrum, the bootstrap assumes independent generator chunks, and near-closure has intrinsic finite-sample ambiguity. \evidence{reports/reviews/2026-11-26-scientific-audit.md}{Audit, simulations, projection evidence and limitations}.

\section{Conclusion}
The observed data reproduce the negative registered H1 decision. The full-vector agreement, false-positive denominator and interpretation of near-complete angular shapes required correction. The amended primary steering hypothesis still requires simulated readouts and actual model interventions with the registered controls. \evidence{results/science/h6_datasets.csv}{Steering eligibility and unavailable measurements}.
